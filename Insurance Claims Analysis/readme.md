# 🚗 Insurance Claims Analysis (Tableau)

## 📌 Overview
This project analyzes a car insurance claims dataset to identify patterns in claim frequency and claim amounts. The goal was to uncover key risk factors and present insights through an interactive Tableau dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Objective
- Identify high-risk customer segments  
- Analyze factors affecting claim frequency and claim amount  
- Build a clear and interactive dashboard for data-driven insights  

---

## 📊 Dataset
The dataset contains ~7,000+ records with the following features:

- **Customer Details:** Age, Gender, Education, Occupation, Marital Status  
- **Financial Information:** Income, Home Value  
- **Vehicle Information:** Car Type, Car Age, Car Usage  
- **Driving & Lifestyle Factors:** Kids Driving, Travel Time, Urban vs Rural  
- **Claims Data:**  
  - Claim Frequency (CLM_FREQ)  
  - Claim Amount (CLM_AMT)  
  - Previous Claims (OLDCLAIM)  

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Tableau (Data Visualization & Dashboarding)  
- Microsoft Excel (Data Source)  

---

## 📈 Key Analysis
- Compared claim frequency and total claim amount across occupations  
- Analyzed claim behavior based on car type and usage  
- Evaluated the relationship between income, home value, and claims  
- Compared claim trends between urban and rural customers  
- Built an interactive dashboard for easy exploration  

---

## 🔍 Key Insights
- Customers with previous claims tend to file more claims  
- Certain occupations contribute more to total claim amounts  
- Urban customers show higher claim activity  
- Older vehicles are associated with increased claim frequency  
- Travel time and lifestyle factors have an impact on claims
