# Scholarship Application Portal – Business Analysis Case Study

A fictional case study planning an online portal to replace a manual, email-and-spreadsheet scholarship application process. All organizations, people and data are invented for practice.

**My role:** Business Analyst

## Deliverables

1. [Overview, stakeholders and process maps](1_Overview_and_Process_Maps.pdf) – problem statement, goals, scope, stakeholders, current- and future-state workflows
2. [Business Requirements Document](2_BRD.pdf) – 12 business rules, 18 prioritized functional requirements, non-functional requirements
3. [User stories](3_User_Stories.pdf) – 10 user stories with Given/When/Then acceptance criteria
4. [Wireframes](4_Wireframes.pdf) – 4 low-fidelity screens for students, staff and committee
5. [UAT test cases](5_UAT_Test_Cases.pdf) – 18 test cases traced to user stories, with defect severity and exit criteria

**Skills shown:** requirements gathering, process mapping, BRD writing, user stories, wireframing, UAT planning
