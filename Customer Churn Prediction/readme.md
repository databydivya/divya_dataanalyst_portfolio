# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis

## 🔍 About the Project

In this project, I worked on analyzing customer data from an e-commerce company to understand why customers are leaving (churn) and what can be done to reduce it.

Instead of just building models, I focused more on **understanding the data, finding patterns, and generating useful business insights**.

---

## 🎯 What I Tried to Solve

Customer churn is a big problem because losing one account can mean losing multiple customers.

So my main goals were:

* To identify which customers are more likely to churn
* To understand the reasons behind churn
* To suggest practical ways the business can retain customers

---

## 📊 About the Data

* Around **11,000+ customer records**
* **19 features** including customer behavior, usage, and demographics
* Target variable: **Churn**

---

## 🛠️ What I Did

### 🧹 Data Cleaning

* Removed unnecessary columns like AccountId
* Handled missing values
* Fixed incorrect data types
* Treated outliers

### 📈 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

I spent most of the time exploring the data:

* Checked distributions of variables
* Identified skewed data
* Compared churn vs non-churn customers
* Used visualizations like bar charts, histograms, and heatmaps

---

## 🔑 What I Found

Some interesting patterns I noticed:

* 📱 Customers using **mobile devices** tend to churn more
* 💳 **Debit card and e-wallet users** have higher churn rates
* 👨 Male customers churn slightly more than female customers
* 💰 Customers in the **regular spending category** are more likely to leave
* 📞 Customers who contacted support frequently are at higher risk of churn

Also, the dataset was a bit **imbalanced**, with fewer churned customers compared to non-churned ones.

---

## 📊 Customer Segmentation

I also tried grouping customers using clustering:

* Found **3 main customer segments**
* One group had clearly higher churn behavior

---

## 💡 My Suggestions

Based on the analysis, here’s what I think the company can do:

* Focus on customers who raise frequent complaints
* Improve experience for mobile users
* Create targeted offers instead of giving discounts to everyone
* Pay attention to regular customers who may quietly churn
